Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 Aug 2026

MindText Mind Map ("the app") is a free, single-page tool for building interactive radial mind maps, with an optional feature to build a map from the contents of a Google Drive folder. This page explains what data the app accesses and what happens to it.

How the app runs

The app is a single static web page. There is no server, no database, and no backend of any kind — everything runs locally in your own browser. Nothing you type or generate in the app is sent to us, because there is no "us" on the receiving end; the app's author cannot see your mind maps, your files, or your Google account data.

Google Drive access

If you use the "Crawl Google Drive" or "Save/Load Drive" features, the app asks you to sign in with Google and requests the drive.file permission scope. This is a narrow, Google-defined scope that only grants access to:

  • Files and folders you explicitly select through Google's own file picker, and
  • Files the app itself creates (for example, the _mindmapfilenotes.txt file it can save back into a folder you picked).

The app cannot see, list, or access any other file in your Google Drive. All communication with Google Drive happens directly between your browser and Google's servers, using an access token that Google issues to your browser — this token, your files, and their contents are never sent to any server operated by the app's author.

What the app does with Drive data

When you crawl a folder, the app reads that folder's file and subfolder listing, file names, and (for generating thumbnails) file content or preview images, all directly from Google's servers to your browser. This is used only to draw the mind map and thumbnails on your screen. If you add notes to files, those notes are saved back into a plain-text file in the same Google Drive folder, under your own Google account, using the same direct browser-to-Google connection.

Other data the app may load

If you add a plain weblink (not a Google Drive file) to a mind map, the app may request a page title and preview image for that link from Microlink, a third-party service, by sending it the URL you entered. No other personal data is sent as part of this request. The app also loads two publicly available code libraries from their standard hosting (Google's own libraries for Drive access, and cdnjs for PDF rendering) — these are code files, not services that receive your data.

Local storage and downloads

Mind maps you create exist only in your browser's memory unless you choose to save them — either as a local file download, or back into your own Google Drive. The app does not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking technology, and does not display ads.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account → Security → Third-party access. Doing so does not delete any files already saved in your Drive.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
